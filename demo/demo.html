Track 1 · Alert triage2 · How a case is decided

Four steps for every case

Case pack: casesalertstransactionscustomersevidence/*.mdprevious_reviewsinternal_list
STEP 1

Explain

explanations.ts

Each document type is matched to transactions on facts: party, amount in pence, account, reference and date.

Invoices, statements, settlement letters, takings books, refunds, payroll…

STEP 2

Look for suspicion

signals.ts

Concrete signals only: a fraud report, a structuring note, a device shared with other customers, a dissolved payer, an invoice that contradicts its payment.

STEP 3

Assess each alert

decision.ts

An alert is covered only when what stays unexplained is below that rule's own threshold.

TM02: unexplained inflow under £2,000 and every material onward payment explained.

STEP 4

Combine

one case, one verdict
ESCALATE on any suspicion
RFI for a gap: one specific request
CLOSE when every alert is covered
submission.ts validates the batch before writing: every case, every alert ID, a verdict-compatible reason code, and cited IDs that exist.

Track 1 · Alert triage4 · Architecture

Four design decisions

1

Check facts, not wording

Every CLOSE path compares party, pence, account, reference and dates. A wrongful close costs 10 points; over-caution costs 2 or 3.

Our audit: shifting the amounts in cited documents by £37 removed 1,561 of 1,827 closes. The rest did not rely on amounts.
2

Deterministic decision logic

Stable ordering and tie-breaks. No randomness, clock or file-order dependence.

Offline, five runs per institution reproduce the output byte for byte, at no model cost.
3

One engine, four institutions

Shared code where the four policies agree. The differences are settings, not forks.

Payments/FX: own-name funding is normal, and £10,000 or more to anyone else needs a matched invoice or contract before release.
4

Fail safe, one case at a time

A malformed record, missing document or thrown error escalates that case with its alert IDs.

Fault injection: each corrupted copy of the Bank folder still wrote all 1,000 lines, with at most one case changed.
Track 1 · Alert triage5 · Optional second pass

A language model as an untrusted reader

Deterministic pass

Every case gets its verdict from the parsers and rules first.

→

Only with a key

The second pass runs when OPENROUTER_API_KEY is set. Without it, nothing changes.

→

Unread documents

The model sees only documents no parser used, on cases they could change.

→

Quote check

A fact counts only if its quote appears word for word in the document.

→

Same rules decide

Checked facts go through the same exact-match rules. The model never picks a verdict.
